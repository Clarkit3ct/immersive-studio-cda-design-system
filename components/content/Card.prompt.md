Use `Card` when content genuinely needs a container. Prefer hairline-separated rows first — this brand boxes very little.

```jsx
<Card eyebrow="Use case" title="Sell the units before they're built.">
  Walk a buyer through a floor plate at full scale months before the shell tops out.
</Card>
```

Flat by design: no shadow ever, 4px radius, hairline border that darkens on hover when `interactive`.
