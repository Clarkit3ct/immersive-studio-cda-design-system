Use `StepItem` for the five-step session explainer and any other ordered process.

```jsx
<StepItem number="01" title="Upload">Send your drawings. We handle the projection-ready prep.</StepItem>
<StepItem number="02" title="Project">The plan is projected on the floor. Elevations take the wall.</StepItem>
```

The numeral is the icon — never pair it with a glyph. Rows are separated by a top hairline, so the list reads as a schedule rather than as cards.
