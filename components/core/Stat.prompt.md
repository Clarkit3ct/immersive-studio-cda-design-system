Use `Stat` for the proof figures that carry the argument — scale, area, and the cost of a late change.

```jsx
<Stat value="1:1" label="True-scale floor and elevation projection" />
<Stat value="$5K to $25K" label="Typical cost of one field change order" />
```

Figures set Light and large; the label stays 15px in `--text-secondary`. Three across a row, separated by hairlines, is the site's pattern.
