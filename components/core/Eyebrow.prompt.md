Use `Eyebrow` as the quiet label above every section headline — it is on every supplied artifact and every section of the site.

```jsx
<Eyebrow>How a session works</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow wide tone="primary">Full scale | Full clarity</Eyebrow>
```

`wide` switches to the 0.42em letterspacing used on the business card; reserve it for the brand promise, not for section labels.
